Fall Semester • ETH Zurich
Thursdays • Sep 17–Dec 17 • 08:15–10:00 and 18:15–20:00

This course uses an active-classroom format built around hands-on practice, discussion, and collaboration. Most morning sessions pair a 45-minute methods deep-dive with 45 minutes of collaborative work on module projects. Most evening sessions pair a 45-minute critical reflection on the readings with 45 minutes of collaborative project work.

Schedule note: The schedule may change as the course progresses because some content may warrant more time. Any updates will be posted here.

Module deliverables and reflections

Modules 1 and 3, as well as Module 5 (the final project), have presentation-based deliverables and short written reflections. Modules 2 and 4 have written reflections only.

Expectations for reflections:

  1. Share a GitHub repository with the teaching staff that demonstrates your engagement with the module content.
  2. Choose a figure from your module output and explain how you calculated it, how you interpret it, and what limitations or considerations should inform that interpretation.
  3. Write a short, one-paragraph reflection on one technical insight and one critical insight you gained through the module.
