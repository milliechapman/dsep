Fall Semester • ETH Zurich
Thursdays • Sep 17–Dec 17 • 08:15–10:00 and 18:15–20:00

This course primarily uses a flipped-classroom format. Some class time will be used for short lectures and live coding, but most will be dedicated to collective discussion of the readings and co-working on group module projects with the professor and teaching assistants.

Schedule note: The schedule may change as the course progresses because some content may warrant more time. Any updates will be posted here.
